One original dad joke for the Dad Joke Showdown — a father, his son,
and a pair of spare socks.

- `main.py` — the entrypoint. Writes the joke to `/output/entry.txt`,
  validated to the gate's limits (UTF-8, 1–200 characters, not
  whitespace-only).

> My son asked why I always carry spare socks. I said, "In case I get
> cold feet."
